About PolicyGuide USA

PolicyGuide USA is America's independent Property & Casualty consumer intelligence resource. We demystify statutory state insurance rules, audit carrier rate filings, and equip consumers with tools to cut insurance costs.

Our Mission: Empowering US Policyholders

Over the past three years, US auto and homeowners insurance premiums have surged by over 38% nationally. Skyrocketing vehicle repair technologies, extreme weather catastrophes, and complex statutory shifts have made shopping for insurance more confusing and expensive than ever.

PolicyGuide USA was founded with a singular objective: to restore transparency to the American insurance market. We believe policyholders deserve straightforward, data-driven comparisons without aggressive sales calls, misleading promotional discounts, or hidden policy exclusions.

Our Research & Data Methodology

Every rate benchmark, carrier score, and compliance rule published on PolicyGuide USA is rigorously verified against public statutory filings and verified industry datasets:

  • National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC): We review annual NAIC Complaint Indexes to gauge consumer grievance ratios against national market share.
  • AM Best Financial Stability Grades: We only benchmark carriers maintaining an AM Best financial strength grade of "A-" (Excellent) or higher, ensuring claims-paying solvency.
  • State Department of Insurance (DOI) Filings: We cross-reference approved rate revisions and statutory minimum limits directly from state regulatory bulletins.
  • Consumer Sample Baselines: Rate estimations reflect standardized sample driver profiles (e.g., 35-year-old clean driving record, 12,000 annual miles, 100/300/100 coverage tier).

For a comprehensive look at how our scoring rubric operates, please read our dedicated Editorial Standards & Methodology.

Editorial Independence & Business Model

PolicyGuide USA is an independent research publication. We are not an insurance agency, underwriter, or broker. We do not issue insurance policies or favor any particular carrier.

To keep our educational guides, state directories, and calculators 100% free for all consumers, we partner with affiliate networks and licensed insurance aggregators. However, advertiser compensation never dictates our editorial opinions, rate filings data, or carrier review ratings.
