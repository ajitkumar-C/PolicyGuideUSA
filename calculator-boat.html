Federal Waterway Mandates: Fuel Spill & Wreckage

Under the federal Oil Pollution Act, the owner of any vessel that leaks fuel into US waters is strictly liable for the cleanup costs—which easily exceed $250,000 for small spills. Ensure your boat insurance policy includes at least $500,000 in fuel spill cleanup liability and professional wreckage removal.
