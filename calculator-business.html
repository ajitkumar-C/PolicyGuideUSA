Core Coverages Every US Business Needs

  • General Liability (CGL): Protects against third-party bodily injury (e.g. client slips and falls on premises) and property damage caused during operations.
  • Commercial Property: Replaces office computers, specialized inventory, and equipment stolen, burned, or damaged by wind.
  • Workers' Compensation: Legally required in almost all 50 states as soon as you hire your first employee to cover medical bills and lost wages for workplace accidents.
