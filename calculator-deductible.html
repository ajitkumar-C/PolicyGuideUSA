The Golden Rule of Deductibles

The average American driver files an insurance collision claim once every 18 years. If your break-even period is under 2.5 years (30 months), raising your deductible from $500 to $1,000 is almost always a mathematically superior choice—provided you keep $1,000 cash in reserve.
