💡 How to Ensure Discounts Are Actually Applied

When policies renew automatically, insurance algorithms frequently omit discounts that require re-verification—such as academic report cards, course certificates, or annual odometer photos.

  • Always request your Declarations Page ("Dec Page"): Review the itemized "Discounts Applied" table on page 2.
  • Re-verify every 12 months: Upload your student's transcript or send your defensive driving PDF as soon as your renewal notice arrives.
  • Ask about occupational credits: Many carriers (such as Farmers, Liberty Mutual, and GEICO) offer 5% discounts for engineers, teachers, nurses, and military veterans.
