Choosing the Right Metal Tier (Bronze vs. Silver vs. Gold)

Under the Affordable Care Act, health plans are categorized into metal tiers based on actuarial value—how you and your plan split medical expenses:

  • Bronze (60/40 Split): Best for healthy individuals who rarely visit doctors and only want catastrophic protection against hospitalization.
  • Silver (70/30 Split): The most popular tier. If your household income qualifies, Silver plans unlock federal Cost-Sharing Reductions that slash copays and deductibles.
  • Gold (80/20 Split): Best for individuals or families with regular specialist visits, chronic conditions, or ongoing branded prescriptions.
