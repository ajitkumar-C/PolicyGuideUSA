Understanding Replacement Cost vs. Market Value

Many homeowners mistakenly believe their insurance coverage should equal their Zillow home value. However, market value includes the land, which cannot be destroyed by fire, wind, or hail. Your insurance policy covers Dwelling Replacement Cost—the pure labor and material expense required to rebuild your house from the ground up after a total loss.

Important Policy Clauses to Verify

  • Extended Replacement Cost (125% to 150%): Provides a financial buffer if surge pricing drives up regional labor and lumber costs after a catastrophe.
  • Separate Hurricane / Windstorm Deductible: In coastal states (like Florida, Texas, and Louisiana), wind damage is often subject to a 2% to 5% percentage deductible rather than a flat dollar amount.
  • Water Backup Endorsement: Standard policies exclude sewer or drain backups; adding this rider costs ~$35/year and protects finished basements and ground floors.
