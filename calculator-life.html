The DIME Formula for Calculating Coverage

Financial planners recommend using the DIME formula to calculate the exact death benefit your family needs:

  • D — Debt: Credit card balances, car loans, and student loans you want wiped out immediately.
  • I — Income: Multiply your current gross annual salary by 10 to 12 years so your spouse and dependents maintain their quality of life.
  • M — Mortgage: The entire remaining principal payoff balance on your home mortgage.
  • E — Education: Projected tuition for each child (estimate ~$100k–$150k per child for 4-year in-state college).
