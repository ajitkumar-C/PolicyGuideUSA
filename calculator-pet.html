Understanding Pre-Existing Conditions

No pet insurance company in the United States covers pre-existing medical conditions. If your dog or cat was diagnosed with allergies, hip dysplasia, or cancer before purchasing a policy, treatments related to that condition are permanently excluded. This is why pet owners are strongly urged to lock in coverage while their pet is still a puppy or kitten.
