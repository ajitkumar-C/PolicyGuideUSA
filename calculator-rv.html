Crucial Distinctions in RV Insurance

  • Motorhomes (Drivable): Require separate state liability insurance because they possess an engine and operate on highways independently.
  • Travel Trailers (Towed): While liability extends from your tow vehicle while driving, you MUST have an RV policy for physical damage, overturns, hail, and campsite injuries when parked.
  • Full-Timer Liability: If your RV is your primary residence, standard recreational policies deny claims. Full-timer endorsements mirror homeowner personal liability.
