When Is Travel Insurance Worth It?

As a rule of thumb, travel insurance is recommended when:

  • You Are Traveling Internationally: Foreign hospitals often require upfront payment or proof of private international medical insurance before admitting patients.
  • Prepaid Costs Are Non-Refundable: Cruises, safaris, and transatlantic airfares usually offer zero refund within 30 to 60 days of departure.
  • You Have Elderly Relatives or Children: Illness of a family member is a standard covered peril allowing full trip cost recovery.
