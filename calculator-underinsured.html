Why State Minimum Limits Are Dangerously Outdated

In states like California, statutory property damage limits were set decades ago at just $5,000. In Florida and New York, it is only $10,000. The average new vehicle in America now costs over $48,000. If you accidentally rear-end a Tesla or cause a multi-vehicle pileup, your insurer pays $10,000 and leaves you personally responsible for the remaining $38,000 balance.
