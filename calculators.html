Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are PolicyGuide US insurance calculators?

Our calculators use official 2026 NAIC financial filings, Highway Loss Data Institute statistics, and state insurance commission rate exhibits. While actual binding rates vary based on individual credit scores, telematics scores, and exact garaging address, our benchmarks provide within 8% to 12% accuracy of final underwritten premiums.

Do I need to enter private personal information to use these tools?

No. Every calculator and audit checklist on PolicyGuide US runs anonymously directly inside your browser. We never require Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, or credit card information.

Why should I choose independent calculators over insurance agent quotes?

Captive insurance agents represent a single company and have financial incentives to recommend products their carrier offers. PolicyGuide US is completely independent, comparing multi-carrier risk bands to uncover coverage gaps before you speak with an agent.
