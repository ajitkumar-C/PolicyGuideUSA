Tool #1

"Am I Underinsured?" Coverage Limit & Umbrella Advisor

Most drivers carry state minimum limits, unaware that an at-fault crash can lead to wage garnishment or home liens. Tell us your profile to find your safe coverage threshold.

💡 Why State Minimum Limits Are Dangerous:

In California, state minimum property damage is only $5,000. In Florida and New York, it is only $10,000. If you accidentally rear-end an electric vehicle or cause a multi-vehicle crash, repair costs routinely reach $40,000+. Your insurance carrier only pays up to your limit; the other party can sue you directly for the remaining balance.

Tool #2

Deductible Savings Break-Even Simulator

Raising your collision and comprehensive deductible lowers your monthly bill. Use this calculator to see how many months you must drive claim-free for the premium savings to outweigh the out-of-pocket risk.

💡 The Golden Rule of Deductibles:

Only raise your deductible to $1,000 or $1,500 if you have that amount set aside in an emergency fund. If an accident occurs, you must pay your deductible before the insurance company pays a single dollar toward repairs.
