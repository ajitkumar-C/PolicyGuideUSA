1. Chubb at a Glance: White-Glove Luxury P&C

Chubb is the world’s largest publicly traded Property & Casualty insurer, operating in 54 countries with over $200 billion in assets. In personal lines, Chubb is the undisputed market leader for high-net-worth individuals and affluent families through its flagship Masterpiece® policy suite.

Unlike mass-market carriers like State Farm or GEICO, Chubb does not compete on bargain pricing. Instead, it provides bespoke, comprehensive coverage tailored to high-value assets, fine art, luxury yachts, and multi-million dollar umbrella liability.

Policies are sold exclusively through premier independent insurance brokerages.

2. Chubb Masterpiece Homeowners: Why Affluent Owners Choose It

Standard homeowners policies from mainline carriers place strict sub-limits on luxury fixtures, imported stonework, and custom millwork. Chubb Masterpiece eliminates these restrictions:

• Extended Rebuilding Cost: Chubb will pay whatever it takes to restore your luxury home to its exact original architectural craftsmanship, even if it exceeds your stated policy limit by 100% or more.

• Cash-Out Option: If your residence is destroyed by fire or natural catastrophe, you can choose to take a full cash settlement rather than rebuild on the same parcel of land.

• Wildfire Defense Services: In western states, Chubb dispatches private certified firefighting crews to spray fire-retardant gel on your home when wildfires approach.

3. Chubb Masterpiece Auto: Agreed Value & OEM Guarantee

Insuring luxury and exotic vehicles through Chubb eliminates standard depreciation battles:

• Agreed Value Coverage: You and Chubb agree on the exact value of your vehicle when the policy is written. If the vehicle is totaled, Chubb writes a check for that exact dollar amount without deducting depreciation.

• Choice of Repair Facility: You can have your Porsche, Ferrari, or Mercedes repaired at any dealership or certified specialty restoration shop of your choice.

• Rental Car Reimbursement Up to $15,000: Chubb covers up to $15,000 for luxury rental cars with no daily dollar cap while your primary vehicle is being repaired.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Chubb only insure million-dollar homes?

While Chubb specializes in homes valued at $1 million or more, they also insure high-value condos, vintage classic cars, and personal umbrella liability policies starting at $5 million limits.

What is Chubb’s cash settlement option?

If your home is destroyed by a covered peril, Chubb gives you the option to receive a cash payout for the full insured value instead of forcing you to rebuild the home on the original site.

Does Chubb offer OEM parts on car insurance claims?

Yes. Chubb guarantees that all auto repairs will use original manufacturer (OEM) parts rather than aftermarket or salvaged parts.

How does Chubb’s Wildfire Defense Service work?

In wildfire-prone states, Chubb contracts private certified firefighting teams who monitor active fire perimeters and apply fire-retardant gel to enrolled homes if flames threaten the property.
