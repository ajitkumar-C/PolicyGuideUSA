1. Erie Insurance at a Glance: Regional Excellence

Founded in 1925 in Erie, Pennsylvania, Erie Insurance Exchange operates as a regional powerhouse covering 12 states: DC, IL, IN, KY, MD, NY, NC, OH, PA, TN, VA, WV, and WI.

Despite its regional focus, Erie is the 12th largest auto insurer in the US. It operates exclusively through independent agents, delivering high-touch customer care at prices that routinely undercut national direct brands.

Its signature feature—Erie Rate Lock—has become one of the most celebrated policy perks in the P&C industry.

2. The Erie Rate Lock Advantage: Truly Frozen Premiums

Unlike traditional auto policies that re-rate every 6 or 12 months based on state-level inflation filing adjustments, Erie Rate Lock® guarantees that your auto premium remains 100% frozen year after year.

Your rate will only change if you make one of three structural changes:

1. You add or remove a driver from your policy.

2. You add or replace a vehicle.

3. You change your primary residential garage address.

This means rate inflation, filing surcharges, and even at-fault accidents will not trigger an automatic rate hike under the Rate Lock endorsement.

4. ErieSecure Home: Guaranteed Replacement Cost Rebuilding

Erie’s homeowners policies feature Guaranteed Replacement Cost. If disaster strikes, Erie pays 100% of the cost to rebuild your home to its previous condition, even if reconstruction costs exceed your dwelling policy limits by tens of thousands of dollars due to post-storm contractor inflation.

Other built-in perks include $5,000 for lost or stolen luggage and zero deductible on small property repairs.

5. Claims & Solvency Ratings

Erie holds an A+ (Superior) financial strength rating from A.M. Best. In J.D. Power’s Auto Insurance Satisfaction Study, Erie frequently claims the #1 overall spot in the Mid-Atlantic and North Central regions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which states does Erie Insurance operate in?

Erie is available in 12 states and D.C.: Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Maryland, New York, North Carolina, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Virginia, West Virginia, and Wisconsin.

Does Erie Rate Lock really prevent rate increases after an accident?

Yes. Unless you change a vehicle, driver, or address, Erie Rate Lock maintains your premium, shielding you from inflation and claim surcharges.

What is Erie YourTurn telematics?

Erie YourTurn is a rewards-based telematics app. Instead of policy discounts, safe drivers earn cash rewards or gift cards (up to $5 to $10 every two weeks) redeemable for Amazon or Walmart vouchers.

Does Erie offer Guaranteed Replacement Cost on home insurance?

Yes. ErieSecure Home offers true Guaranteed Replacement Cost, paying whatever it takes to rebuild your home to original specs with no artificial percentage cap.
