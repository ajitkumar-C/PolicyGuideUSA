1. The Battle of the Direct Insurance Pioneers

GEICO and Progressive built their modern empires by cutting out expensive agent brick-and-mortar commissions and passing savings directly to policyholders via telephone and online quoting.

Today, GEICO (backed by Berkshire Hathaway) and Progressive battle fiercely for the #2 and #3 auto insurance market share spots in America.

3. DriveEasy vs. Snapshot Telematics

Both carriers push telematics heavily to reward safe drivers:

• GEICO DriveEasy: Monitored continuously through the GEICO app. It evaluates braking, acceleration, cornering, and phone interaction. Caution: DriveEasy will increase rates in most states for drivers with low safety scores.

• Progressive Snapshot: Available as a plug-in OBD device or mobile app. Offers an average $156 annual discount, but roughly 18% of participants see a premium surcharge upon completing their tracking period.

4. The Homeowners Bundling Trap

Neither GEICO nor Progressive underwrites the majority of their homeowners policies under their primary corporate balance sheets:

• GEICO acts purely as an agency broker, routing homeowners policies to third-party partners like Travelers, Homesite, Liberty Mutual, or Chubb.

• Progressive underwrites through its subsidiary ASI (Progressive Home) in select markets, but also uses HomeQuote Explorer to broker other carriers.

While you receive a multi-policy discount on your auto policy from both, you may deal with two separate claims adjusters if a storm damages both your house and vehicle.

5. The Verdict: How to Choose

• Go with GEICO if: You have a clean driving record, work for the federal government or military, and prioritize the lowest possible direct auto premium.

• Go with Progressive if: You have tickets or accidents on your record, want the Name Your Price tool, or prefer independent agent support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GEICO cheaper than Progressive for car insurance?

Yes, for clean-record drivers and federal employees, GEICO is typically 7% to 10% cheaper than Progressive. However, if you have tickets or prior accidents, Progressive is often significantly cheaper.

Can GEICO DriveEasy raise my rate?

Yes. In most states, GEICO DriveEasy can increase your insurance premium if your tracked driving behavior displays harsh braking, late-night driving, or phone distraction.

Does GEICO write its own homeowners insurance?

No. GEICO Insurance Agency partners with third-party carriers like Travelers and Homesite to provide home insurance, although the multi-policy discount applies to your auto rate.

Who has better mobile app functionality?

Both carriers have award-winning 4.8-star mobile apps offering instant digital ID cards, roadside assistance tracking, and mobile claims filing.
