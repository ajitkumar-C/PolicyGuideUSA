1. Nationwide at a Glance: "On Your Side" Protection

Founded in 1926 as the Farm Bureau Mutual Automobile Insurance Company, Nationwide has grown into a Fortune 100 powerhouse offering comprehensive Property & Casualty, pet insurance, commercial liability, and retirement solutions.

Nationwide transitioned from a traditional captive-agent model into an independent broker partnership system, combining digital direct-quote convenience with independent agency consultations.

Because Nationwide is a mutual company, policyholder interests take priority over quarterly stock earnings, resulting in high customer retention and reliable policy renewals.

3. SmartRide vs. SmartMiles: Choosing Your Telematics Option

Nationwide provides two distinct telematics tracks depending on your driving patterns:

1. SmartRide®: A behavioral telematics program that measures acceleration, harsh braking, night driving, and idle engine time over 4 to 6 months. Safe participants earn up to a 40% discount, and Nationwide guarantees your rate will never increase based on SmartRide data.

2. SmartMiles®: A genuine pay-per-mile auto insurance program. You pay a low monthly base rate (e.g., $45) plus a variable per-mile rate (typically 5 to 7 cents per mile). Perfect for individuals who commute by train, work from home, or drive under 500 miles per month.

4. Vanishing Deductible & Better Car Replacement

Nationwide’s standout feature is the Vanishing Deductible® endorsement:

• Upon enrollment, you receive an immediate $100 deductible reduction.

• For each subsequent year of claim-free driving, your deductible drops by another $100 until it hits $0.

• After an accident, your deductible resets by only $100 rather than wiping out your entire accumulated credit.

In addition, Nationwide offers Better Car Replacement®, which pays to replace your totaled vehicle with a model that is one model year newer and has 15,000 fewer miles.

5. Claims Service & A.M. Best Solvency Ratings

Nationwide holds an A+ (Superior) financial strength rating from A.M. Best and an A1 rating from Moody’s. Its On Your Side® Claims Service includes certified repair network guarantees and an annual On Your Side Review to ensure policy limits keep pace with home remodeling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Nationwide SmartRide increase premiums if you drive poorly?

No. Unlike Progressive Snapshot, Nationwide SmartRide has a zero-surcharge guarantee: you can only earn discounts (up to 40%) or keep your baseline rate, without punitive rate hikes.

How does SmartMiles pay-per-mile billing work?

You pay a fixed base monthly rate plus a variable rate per mile driven (e.g., 6 cents/mile). On road trips, only the first 250 miles in a single day are counted, capping vacation driving expenses.

How much can I save by bundling home and auto with Nationwide?

Nationwide policyholders save an average of 18% to 22% by bundling car and home insurance together, translating to $600 to $850 in annual household savings.

Is Nationwide available in all 50 states?

Nationwide operates in 46 states and Washington D.C., with select coastal states restricted for new property underwriting.
