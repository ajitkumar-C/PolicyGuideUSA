State Farm vs. Progressive (2026): Rates, Telematics & Which Insurer Wins?

Executive Summary: 2026 Actuarial Findings

  • Overall Pricing: State Farm is generally cheaper for clean drivers, families, and home-auto bundles; Progressive wins for drivers with tickets or accidents.

1. The Titans of American Auto Insurance

State Farm and Progressive collectively control over 35% of the total US private passenger auto insurance market. While State Farm has held the #1 market share ranking for over eight decades, Progressive is rapidly closing the gap through digital quoting innovation and aggressive rate filing optimization.

Deciding between these two giants comes down to your personal risk profile, customer service preference, and whether you prefer an in-person local agent or a modern digital app experience.

3. Telematics: Drive Safe & Save vs. Progressive Snapshot

Both carriers offer mobile usage-based insurance, but their terms differ sharply:

• State Farm Drive Safe & Save: Saves drivers up to 30%. Crucially, State Farm guarantees that your premium will never increase based on your telematics driving data. It is a discount-only program.

• Progressive Snapshot: Saves daytime drivers an average of $156 per year, but roughly 18% of enrolled drivers receive rate increases if they drive late at night (midnight to 4 AM) or brake hard repeatedly.

4. Homeowners Insurance & Bundling Power

When it comes to property protection, State Farm maintains a distinct advantage. State Farm underwrites and services its own homeowners, condo, and renters policies directly through State Farm Fire & Casualty Company.

In contrast, while Progressive owns American Strategic Insurance (ASI / Progressive Home), in many coastal and high-risk states it refers homeowners policies out to third-party affiliated carriers through HomeQuote Explorer. State Farm’s unified multi-policy discount typically yields greater total household savings.

5. The Verdict: Which Carrier Should You Choose?

• Choose State Farm if: You have a pristine driving record, want a dedicated local agent in your neighborhood, and plan to bundle home and auto policies.

• Choose Progressive if: You have a recent moving violation or minor accident, prefer completely online policy management, or want to customize limits using the Name Your Price tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is State Farm really cheaper than Progressive?

State Farm is generally cheaper for clean drivers and homeowners who bundle. However, Progressive is almost always cheaper for drivers with a speeding ticket, accident, or poor credit.

Can Progressive Snapshot raise my rate while State Farm won’t?

Yes. State Farm Drive Safe & Save has a zero-surcharge guarantee, whereas Progressive Snapshot can and does raise rates for approximately 18% of participants.

Which company has better customer service?

State Farm consistently scores higher in J.D. Power customer satisfaction studies, primarily because of its dedicated network of 19,000+ local neighborhood agents.

Do both companies offer accident forgiveness?

Yes. State Farm offers free accident forgiveness after 9 continuous years of clean driving. Progressive offers small accident forgiveness automatically and sells a Large Accident Forgiveness rider.
