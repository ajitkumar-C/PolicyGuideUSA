1. Travelers at a Glance: 170+ Years of Commercial & Personal Protection

Founded in 1853, The Travelers Companies is one of the oldest and largest Property & Casualty insurance institutions in the United States. Holding the iconic red umbrella trademark, Travelers commands top-tier market share in both commercial insurance and private personal lines.

Unlike captive-agent carriers such as State Farm and Allstate, Travelers operates almost exclusively through an independent insurance agency distribution network. This allows prospective policyholders to consult local independent agents who can shop Travelers alongside competing carriers.

Travelers is especially renowned for its policy flexibility, extensive multi-policy packaging discounts, and specialty endorsements tailored to modern homeowners and vehicle owners.

2. Average Travelers Insurance Rates in 2026

Travelers maintains highly competitive rates for married couples, homeowners who bundle their auto policies, and drivers with clean records. Its pricing tends to be moderately higher for high-risk drivers with DUI convictions or multiple recent moving violations.

3. Travelers IntelliDrive: The 90-Day Telematics Game Changer

One of Travelers’ greatest competitive edges in the telematics space is its IntelliDrive mobile program. While Progressive Snapshot and GEICO DriveEasy require ongoing or lengthy device tracking, Travelers IntelliDrive monitors driving behaviors for only 90 consecutive days.

During this 90-day evaluation, the IntelliDrive mobile app monitors speed, acceleration, braking habits, time of day (midnight driving surcharges), and handheld smartphone interaction.

Once the 90-day evaluation completes, policyholders can earn up to a 30% ongoing discount that remains locked on their policy indefinitely. Travelers also offers IntelliDrivePlus in select states for drivers who prefer continuous mileage tracking for pay-per-mile savings.

4. Premier Endorsements: New Car Replacement & Responsible Driver Plan

Travelers provides some of the strongest vehicle protection riders in the P&C marketplace:

• Premier New Car Replacement®: If your new car is totaled within the first 5 years of ownership, Travelers will pay to replace it with a brand new car of the same make and model, bypassing standard Kelly Blue Book depreciation schedules.

• Premier Responsible Driver Plan®: Includes Accident Forgiveness (one accident forgiven every 36 months) and a Decreasing Deductible that credits $50 toward your collision deductible for every 6-month policy term completed without a violation or accident.

• Travelers Homeowners Protective Add-ons: Offers Green Home replacement (paying extra to rebuild with eco-friendly Energy Star materials) and Water Backup & Sump Overflow riders up to $25,000.

5. Claims Handling, J.D. Power Scores & Solvency

In J.D. Power’s annual Property Claims and Auto Claims satisfaction studies, Travelers consistently earns solid middle-to-high scores (865/1,000), praised for quick digital estimates via its Virtual Visits video appraisal tool.

From a financial balance sheet perspective, Travelers holds an A++ (Superior) rating from A.M. Best and an AA rating from Standard & Poor’s. Its enterprise balance sheet can easily withstand catastrophic weather claim waves without threatening policyholder payouts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Travelers IntelliDrive raise my insurance rates?

Yes, in most states, risky driving behavior (excessive speeding, hard braking, or phone distractions) during the 90-day tracking window can result in a rate increase of up to 15%. However, safe daytime drivers save up to 30% permanently.

How does Travelers compare to State Farm?

Travelers sells through independent agents who can quote multiple carriers, whereas State Farm uses captive agents selling only State Farm. Travelers excels in 90-day telematics and 5-year new car replacement, while State Farm often wins on standalone auto discounts.

What is the Travelers Decreasing Deductible perk?

Through the Premier Responsible Driver Plan, Travelers deducts $50 from your collision deductible for each consecutive 6-month policy term completed without a crash or moving violation, up to a maximum deduction of $500.

Does Travelers offer roadside assistance?

Yes, Travelers offers 24/7 roadside assistance including towing up to 15 miles, battery jump-starts, flat tire changes, fuel delivery, and lockout services starting at approximately $18 to $30 per vehicle per year.
