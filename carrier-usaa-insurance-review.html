1. USAA at a Glance: The Gold Standard in Personal P&C

Founded in 1922 by a group of 25 Army officers in San Antonio who were unable to secure affordable auto insurance, USAA has become America’s preeminent military financial institution.

USAA consistently ranks #1 in virtually every independent consumer study for customer loyalty, net promoter score, and claims satisfaction.

Because USAA operates exclusively for military families and does not pay dividends to external Wall Street stockholders, it returns capital to members via Subscriber Savings Accounts and maintains exceptionally low expense ratios.

3. USAA SafePilot Telematics: 30% Savings With Zero Risk

USAA’s SafePilot® mobile app evaluates harsh braking, handheld phone calling, hands-free phone calling, and phone interaction while driving.

Drivers receive an immediate 10% enrollment discount. Upon policy renewal, drivers can earn up to a 30% discount based on their cumulative driving score.

Critically, USAA SafePilot guarantees that low scores will never increase your base insurance rates, giving military members a completely risk-free discount opportunity.

4. Dedicated Military Perks: Deployment Discounts & Uniform Coverage

USAA builds specialized military provisions directly into its P&C policies at zero surcharge:

• Vehicle Storage During Deployment: Save up to 60% on auto premiums when a vehicle is placed in secure base storage during overseas deployment.

• Zero Homeowners Deductible on Military Gear: Standard USAA homeowners and renters policies waive deductibles entirely for military uniforms and gear lost, stolen, or damaged during active duty.

• On-Base Garaging Discount: Save up to 15% on collision and comprehensive coverage if your vehicle is garaged inside a military installation.

5. Who Qualifies for USAA Membership?

Because of its non-profit military mission, membership is restricted to:

1. Active duty members of the U.S. Air Force, Army, Coast Guard, Marines, Navy, and Space Force.

2. National Guard and Reserve personnel.

3. Honorably discharged veterans and military retirees.

4. Cadets and midshipmen at U.S. service academies or in advanced ROTC programs.

5. Direct spouses and children of existing USAA members (membership passes down through generations).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can civilians get USAA insurance?

No. Civilians without military heritage cannot buy USAA auto or property insurance. Membership requires active duty service, veteran status, or being the child or spouse of an existing member.

Does USAA SafePilot raise rates for bad drivers?

No. USAA SafePilot only applies discounts (up to 30%). It will not raise your premium if you log poor driving scores.

What is the USAA vehicle deployment discount?

If you are deployed and store your car in an approved, secure location for at least 30 consecutive days, USAA discounts your auto premium by up to 60%.

What is USAA’s A.M. Best financial strength rating?

USAA holds an A++ (Superior) rating from A.M. Best—the highest possible grade—confirming bulletproof solvency and claims reserves.
