Frequently Asked Questions

Everyday Insurance Questions Answered

Which car insurance company is cheapest in the US?

For drivers with clean records, GEICO and Erie Insurance (in the 12 states it serves) consistently rank among the most affordable. For drivers with recent speeding tickets, an at-fault collision, or continuous insurance gaps, Progressive often provides the most competitive quotes.

Why are state minimum coverage limits considered dangerous?

State minimums (like 25/50/25 in Texas or California) only cover up to $25,000 in property damage. Since the average new car costs over $48,000, hitting a luxury car, SUV, or causing a multi-car chain reaction can leave you with $30,000+ in out-of-pocket debt. Carrying at least 100/300/100 liability protects your home and savings.

What is the difference between Snapshot and Drive Safe & Save?

State Farm's Drive Safe & Save is a "discount-only" program in almost every state—if you drive poorly, you won't get a discount, but your base rate won't go up. Progressive's Snapshot, however, can add a surcharge (rate hike) to your policy if it records frequent hard braking, late-night driving, or rapid acceleration.
